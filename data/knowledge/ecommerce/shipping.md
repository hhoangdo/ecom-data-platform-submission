# Shipping policy

Shipping estimates begin after payment approval and address validation. An estimate is not a guaranteed delivery appointment because carrier capacity, weather, customs review, remote delivery routes, and an incorrect address can change the actual arrival time. The order confirmation shows the selected delivery service, destination, and any delivery charge. Customers should review those details immediately because the fulfillment team may begin packing soon after payment is accepted.

Standard delivery is available only to supported service areas. Express delivery is available when the checkout page presents that option for the item, destination, and local carrier schedule. Large, hazardous, temperature sensitive, or marketplace fulfilled items can have different delivery options and may require additional handling time. When an order contains items with different readiness dates, the order may arrive in separate parcels. The order history lists every parcel and its carrier tracking number when tracking becomes available.

Customers are responsible for providing a complete address, reachable contact information, and any building or access details required for delivery. A carrier may contact the customer for an appointment, identity check, or pickup instruction. If a parcel is returned because the address was incomplete, refused, or not collected after notice, support can explain the available resend or refund options. Additional shipping charges can apply when a customer requests a resend after an address error.

The delivery record is normally the carrier scan or delivery confirmation. If a parcel is delayed, customers should first check the order history for the latest carrier event. Support can open a carrier inquiry when tracking has not progressed for a reasonable period or when the scan indicates a delivery exception. A missing parcel report should include the order number, the delivery address, and any safe place or building information that can help the carrier investigate.

Shipping fees, delivery zones, and cutoff times are displayed before the customer places the order. Promotions can change a delivery fee only when their terms explicitly say so. Customs duties, local taxes, or import requirements imposed by an authority are not carrier service fees and are handled according to the destination rules. Support does not promise a refund or replacement before the carrier inquiry and order facts are reviewed.
