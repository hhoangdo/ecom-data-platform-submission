# Warranties policy

Warranty coverage depends on the product, seller, manufacturer, destination, and the written warranty supplied with the item. The product page may summarize available coverage, but the included manufacturer documentation and mandatory consumer law control where they provide stronger rights. Customers should keep the order confirmation, serial number, and proof of delivery because those records help identify the item and applicable coverage period.

A warranty request should describe the fault, when it began, the troubleshooting already attempted, and any visible damage. Support may request photographs, a serial number, diagnostic information, or a return inspection before deciding whether the claim is covered. Customers should not open, repair, alter, or discard an item when that could prevent a safe inspection. If safety is a concern, customers should stop using the item and follow the manufacturer safety instructions.

Warranty coverage generally applies to defects in materials or workmanship during the stated coverage period. It does not normally cover accidental damage, misuse, normal wear, consumable parts, cosmetic changes that do not affect function, unauthorized repair, loss, theft, or damage caused by an external service. A claim may also be excluded when the product was used outside the intended environment or with incompatible equipment contrary to the documentation.

When a covered claim is approved, the available remedy can be repair, replacement, refund, or another remedy required by law or the warranty terms. A replacement may be a comparable item when the original is unavailable. Repair or replacement processing times depend on parts, inspection, and carrier handling. Support communicates the approved remedy and any return instructions before asking the customer to ship an item.

Extended protection plans and marketplace seller warranties are separate from the platform policy unless the order expressly identifies the platform as the administrator. Their provider, duration, deductible, registration, and claim process are shown in the related terms. Customers can contact support for order records, but support cannot promise coverage that is not included in the applicable written warranty.
