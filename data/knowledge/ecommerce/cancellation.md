# Cancellation policy

Customers can request cancellation from the order history while an order is still marked as pending, confirmed, or preparing. The cancellation control is shown only when the fulfillment system can still accept a request. A request made by email, chat, or phone after the control is unavailable may be reviewed by support, but it cannot be promised because warehouse processing can continue between messages. The order status is the authoritative record for whether cancellation remains possible.

An order that has been packed, handed to a carrier, or marked shipped cannot usually be cancelled. In that situation, customers should follow the returns policy after delivery if the item is eligible. Refusing a parcel is not the same as a confirmed cancellation and can delay the review of a refund. Customers should not dispose of a delivered item or its packaging before support confirms the appropriate next step.

When a cancellation succeeds before shipment, the payment authorization or charge is released or refunded to the original payment method. Bank processing times vary and are controlled by the payment provider. A payment confirmation may remain visible while a pending authorization is being reversed. Support can provide the cancellation reference and the recorded cancellation time, but cannot change a bank settlement schedule.

Marketplace items, made to order goods, digital products, services with a scheduled delivery, and orders funded by a promotion can have additional restrictions. The product page and checkout terms identify those restrictions. A seller may need to confirm an unshipped marketplace cancellation, while the platform records the request and notifies the customer of the result. A cancellation does not reserve inventory or preserve the price of a later replacement order.

If only one item in a multi item order is cancelled, the remaining items continue unless the order record says otherwise. Delivery fees, promotions, bundle discounts, and tax calculations can be recalculated after a partial cancellation. The customer receives an updated order summary showing what remains active, what was cancelled, and any refund or authorization release that was initiated.
