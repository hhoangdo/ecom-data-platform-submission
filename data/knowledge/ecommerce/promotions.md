# Promotions policy

Promotions are available only during the stated period and only while the published stock, account, location, and product conditions are met. The offer details shown on the product page, promotion page, checkout, or a valid customer message describe the eligible items, minimum purchase requirement, redemption method, and expiration. A promotion is not reserved until the order is accepted. Inventory and eligibility can change before checkout is completed.

Unless the offer states otherwise, one promotion code may be used per order and promotion codes cannot be exchanged for cash, credit, or a price adjustment after an order is accepted. Codes may be limited to one use per customer, household, device, payment method, or account. The platform can refuse a code that was obtained through an unauthorized reseller, automated activity, account abuse, or a technical error that clearly conflicts with the published offer.

Discounts are calculated from the eligible item price before any excluded services, taxes, delivery charges, duties, gift cards, or other amounts identified in the offer. A bundle, threshold, or buy one get one offer can change when an item is removed, returned, or cancelled. If a qualifying item is returned, the refund is based on the amount actually paid after the promotion allocation. Support can explain the calculation shown on the order, but cannot apply a different discount that was not valid at checkout.

Free delivery promotions apply only to the delivery service, destination, and order value named in the offer. They do not normally cover premium delivery upgrades, remote area surcharges, customs charges, or a resend caused by an address error. Marketplace sellers may have separate promotion terms when their listing identifies a seller funded offer. The applicable seller or platform terms are displayed with the item before purchase.

The platform may correct an obvious pricing, display, or technical error and may cancel an affected order when required by law or the offer terms. Customers receive notice of a correction or cancellation and, when appropriate, a refund or authorization release. Promotion abuse, including account creation solely to evade a limit, can result in the removal of the promotion and review of affected orders.
