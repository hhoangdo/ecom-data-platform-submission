# Privacy policy

The service uses customer information to operate the account, process orders, deliver products, provide support, prevent fraud, meet legal obligations, and improve the reliability of the shopping experience. Information can include contact details, delivery information, order history, payment status, device and browser signals, support messages, and preferences that a customer chooses to provide. The platform collects only information needed for the stated purpose and applies access controls appropriate to the service.

Payment card details are handled by approved payment providers rather than stored in ordinary support records. Customers should never send passwords, full card numbers, security codes, or one time verification codes to support. If a message asks for information that does not match the normal secure checkout or account flow, customers should stop and use an official support channel. Support agents can verify an order through approved account checks without requesting sensitive payment credentials in a message.

Information may be shared with service providers that perform delivery, payment processing, fraud prevention, customer support, analytics, hosting, or legal compliance functions. Providers are expected to use the information only for the services they perform and under applicable contractual and legal controls. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect customers or the platform from fraud or security threats, or as part of a lawful business transaction.

Customers can review and update account details through the account settings when that feature is available. Requests to access, correct, delete, restrict, or export personal information are handled according to applicable law and may require identity verification. Some information must be retained for order records, fraud prevention, tax, accounting, warranty, dispute, or legal obligations even when an account is closed or a deletion request is accepted.

The service uses cookies and similar technologies for sign in, cart continuity, security, preference storage, measurement, and relevant service improvements. Customers can manage browser controls, although disabling necessary cookies can limit account or checkout functions. This policy describes general handling; a customer should use the jurisdiction specific notice or contact channel provided by the service when local law grants additional rights.
