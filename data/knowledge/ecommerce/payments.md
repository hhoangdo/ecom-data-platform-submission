# Payments policy

Checkout accepts only the payment methods displayed for the customer location and order. Availability can depend on currency, order value, fraud checks, product type, and the payment provider. Customers must use a payment method they are authorized to use and must provide accurate billing information. The platform does not ask customers to send card numbers, one time codes, passwords, or bank credentials through email, chat, or social media.

Payment approval can create an authorization before fulfillment begins. An authorization confirms that the payment provider approved the transaction amount at that time; it does not always mean the final settlement has completed. If an order cannot be accepted, is cancelled, or changes before settlement, the provider may release or reverse the authorization. The time shown by a banking application can differ from the order status because each provider processes reversals on its own schedule.

Customers should review the order total before placing an order. The total can include item prices, taxes, delivery charges, discounts, credits, and other amounts shown at checkout. A promotion applies only when the order meets its terms at the moment the order is placed. Removing an item, cancelling part of an order, or changing an address can cause the final eligible discount or delivery charge to change. The updated order summary is the record of the resulting amount.

For security, the platform may decline, hold, or request verification for an order when payment information, billing details, or order activity cannot be validated. Support can explain the next safe step but cannot override a payment provider decision or reveal fraud screening rules. Customers should contact their bank or provider if a payment is declined and should not repeatedly submit the same order with changed information unless the provider has resolved the issue.

Refunds are issued only after the applicable cancellation or return review is approved. They are sent to the original payment method unless law or provider rules require a different handling method. Store credits, gift cards, and promotional balances can be returned according to the terms shown when they were used. Chargeback notices should be handled promptly because an open dispute can limit further account changes while the payment provider investigates.
