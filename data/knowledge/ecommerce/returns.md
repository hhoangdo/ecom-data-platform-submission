# Returns policy

Customers may request a return for an eligible physical item within thirty calendar days after delivery. The return window begins on the delivery date shown by the carrier, not on the order date or the date a customer opens a support request. To start a return, the customer selects the delivered item in the order history, gives a short reason, and confirms a pickup or drop-off choice. A request does not guarantee approval when the item is excluded, damaged by misuse, incomplete, or outside the return window.

Eligible items must be returned with supplied parts, manuals, promotional gifts, and protective packaging when those materials were included. The item should be clean and should not show damage caused after delivery. Apparel may be tried on indoors, but items with removed hygiene seals cannot be returned. Digital products, gift cards, personalized goods, and items clearly marked final sale are not eligible unless the product arrived defective or the order record is wrong.

When a carrier scan confirms receipt, the warehouse checks the return against the original order. Approved refunds use the original payment method. Standard delivery charges are refunded only when the item was defective, damaged in transit, or sent in error. If the customer chose a premium delivery option, the additional delivery charge is not refunded. The customer receives a confirmation email when the refund is issued.

If the warehouse cannot approve the return, support explains the reason and may arrange shipment back to the customer when appropriate. Exchanges are handled as a return followed by a new order, so stock and price may differ from the original purchase. This historical version applies only before the effective transition shown in its metadata.

# Returns policy

Customers may request a return for an eligible physical item within forty five calendar days after delivery. The delivery date recorded by the carrier starts the window. The order history provides the supported return flow, including a reason, the item condition, and a pickup or drop-off option. Opening a request preserves the request record, but approval still depends on item eligibility, condition, and the facts confirmed by the warehouse.

An eligible return includes the item and all supplied components, manuals, accessories, promotional gifts, and protective packaging when available. Normal inspection is allowed, but damage from use, missing serial labels, altered safety labels, or removed hygiene seals can make an item ineligible. Digital products, gift cards, personalized goods, and final sale goods remain excluded unless the delivered item was defective, damaged in transit, or materially different from the order.

After the return carrier scan, the warehouse compares the shipment with the original order. Approved refunds go to the original payment method. Standard delivery charges are refunded for a verified delivery or product error. Premium delivery upgrades are not refunded. The confirmation message states the approved item, refund amount, and the payment method used for the refund.

If a return is declined, support provides the documented reason and a next step. Where a return shipment is sent back, the customer receives tracking information. An exchange is a separate new order after the approved return, so availability, promotion eligibility, and price are evaluated again under current rules.
