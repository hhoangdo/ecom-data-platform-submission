# Marketplace support policy

Marketplace listings are offered by independent sellers through the platform. The listing identifies the seller, the item condition, the delivery estimate, and any seller specific policy that applies. The platform provides ordering, payment, communication, and support tools, while the seller remains responsible for accurately describing its listing and fulfilling obligations assigned to the seller. Customers should review the listing details before purchase because availability, delivery origin, and return eligibility can differ from a platform fulfilled item.

After an order is placed, the seller receives the order details needed to fulfill it. Sellers must use the approved order channel and must not request payment outside the platform. A customer should not send bank transfers, gift card codes, passwords, or personal identity documents directly to a seller unless an official, legally required process clearly identifies the purpose. Messages that attempt to move a transaction outside the platform should be reported to support.

Delivery tracking, seller messages, and order updates appear in the order history when supplied by the seller or carrier. If an order is late, incomplete, damaged, materially different from the listing, or missing, the customer should use the marketplace support option attached to the order. Support reviews the order record, seller response, delivery evidence, and any customer documentation. The platform may ask the customer to keep packaging, provide photographs, or return the item using approved instructions.

Marketplace returns follow the applicable listing and platform rules. A seller cannot reduce statutory consumer rights or the protections shown at checkout. Where a return is approved, the platform provides the available return method and records the tracking information. Refund timing depends on the return evidence, seller response period, payment method, and applicable law. Customers should not send an item to an address supplied only in an unverified message.

The platform may take action when a seller violates listing, fulfillment, safety, communication, or payment rules. Possible actions include pausing a listing, limiting seller tools, cancelling affected orders, or providing a customer remedy where required. Support cannot guarantee a specific outcome before the evidence is reviewed, but it records the case, provides updates through the order channel, and explains the available next step.
